# Reporting a Suspected Phishing Email

Northwind Logistics employees must report any suspicious email within 15 minutes of noticing it. The preferred method is the Report Phish button in the Outlook toolbar, which forwards the message with full headers to the security team and removes it from the user's inbox. If the button is unavailable, employees forward the message as an attachment to phishing@northwindlogistics.example. Forwarding as an attachment preserves the original headers, which analysts need for investigation.

Employees must not click links, open attachments, or reply to the sender before reporting. If an employee already clicked a link or opened an attachment, they must say so in the report and call the IT Service Desk at extension 4400 immediately. Employees are never punished for reporting in good faith, even when they clicked.

Every report creates a ticket in the ServiceDesk Plus queue named SEC-PHISH. The reporter receives an automatic acknowledgment within 5 minutes. The security team aims to send the reporter a verdict of malicious, spam, or safe within 4 business hours. Reports that arrive outside business hours, which run from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern, are picked up by the on-call analyst, who reviews high priority reports within 1 hour.

Employees are trained to look for urgent payment requests, mismatched sender domains, unexpected password reset notices, and attachments with double extensions such as invoice.pdf.exe. Quarterly simulated phishing campaigns measure how many employees report the message rather than click it. The company target is a report rate of at least 70 percent.
