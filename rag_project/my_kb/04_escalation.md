# Escalation and Notification

The phishing response team has four roles. Tier 1 analysts handle triage and containment. Tier 2 analysts handle compromised accounts and devices. The Incident Commander is the on-call senior analyst who coordinates a Severity 1 incident. The CISO is the executive who owns security risk for the company.

Notification depends on severity. For a Severity 1 incident, Tier 1 must notify the Incident Commander within 15 minutes and the CISO within 30 minutes. The Incident Commander opens a bridge call and posts updates in the #sec-incident channel every 60 minutes until the incident is contained. For a Severity 2 incident, the Tier 1 analyst informs the team lead within 1 hour, and the CISO receives a summary in the daily security report. Severity 3 and Severity 4 tickets do not require escalation.

Any phishing email aimed at an executive is automatically treated as at least Severity 2, even if nobody clicked. This is sometimes called whaling, and attackers expect these targets to have broad access.

The Legal team is notified within 2 hours if there is any sign that customer or employee personal data was accessed. Legal then has 24 hours to decide whether breach notification obligations apply. Human Resources is notified when an employee is a repeat clicker or may have acted deliberately. The Communications team is involved only when the CISO decides a public or customer statement is needed.

If a Tier 1 analyst is unsure about a severity rating, the rule is to rate the incident higher and escalate. A rating can always be lowered later, but a delayed escalation cannot be recovered.
