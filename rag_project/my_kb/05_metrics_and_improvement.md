# Metrics and Continuous Improvement

The security team tracks four phishing metrics. Mean time to triage measures the minutes from ticket creation to a verdict, and the target for Severity 1 and Severity 2 tickets is 30 minutes. The report rate is the share of simulated phishing emails that employees report, with a target of at least 70 percent. The click rate is the share of employees who click a simulated phishing link, and the target is below 5 percent. The false positive rate tracks how many reported emails turn out to be safe, and it is used to tune the Report Phish button messaging.

The team meets every Thursday at 10:00 AM to review open tickets, new campaigns, and metric trends. After every Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident, the Incident Commander runs a post incident review within 5 business days. The review is blameless and produces a written list of actions with an owner and a due date.

Employees who click a simulated phishing link are shown a 15 minute microlearning module within 24 hours. An employee who clicks three simulated emails within 12 months has a conversation with their manager and the security awareness lead. Employees who report real phishing emails receive a short thank you message from the security team, because positive feedback keeps the report rate high.

The SOC manager owns this runbook. It is reviewed every 6 months, and it is updated sooner if a post incident review finds a gap. Version history is kept in the company wiki.
